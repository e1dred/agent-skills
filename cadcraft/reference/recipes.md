## Round 5: end-to-end recipe, a metre-unit floor plan on an A3 sheet (2026-10-26, rendered and looked at)
Order that worked in ONE `ccrun.run` list: load linetype, readable `style`, layers with colour and lineweight (WALL 0.5 mm, DOOR, DIM, TEXT, FRAME), `mlstyle {"name":"WALL","elements":[{"offset":0.1},{"offset":-0.1}],"current":true}`, `mline` walls (closed outline plus a partition; `justification:"zero"`, `scale:1` or the default style's ±0.5 offsets apply), a door symbol drawn once in `door.dxf` (line + arc) and placed with `insert {"name":"DOOR90","file":"door.dxf","at","scale":[-1,1]}` (a negative X scale mirrors the swing; this avoids needing handles for `block`), `text` labels, `dimstyle` and `dimlinear`, then `layout.new {"viewport":false}`, `pagesetup`, `layout set`, a `rectang` frame in paper space, `mview {"p1","p2"}`, `viewport.set {"handle":"{last}","scale":…,"center":[x,y]}`, `table`, `exportpdf {"layout"}`. Layer colours and lineweights came out right in the PDF (door green, labels yellow, walls heavier).
Traps seen: (1) **`viewport.set` scale is paper units per model unit**: in a metre drawing 1:20 is `50` (1000 mm / 20); `"1:20"` gave 0.05 and a blank sheet with a dot. A viewport's `viewHeight` in the result tells you what is shown. (2) **Model-space text and dimension heights are in model units**: with a 1:50 viewport a 0.25 m label prints 12.5 mm, far too big; make annotation height = wanted print height ÷ viewport scale (set `DIMTXT`, `DIMASZ`, `DIMEXO`, `DIMEXE` and the text heights accordingly, or use `DIMSCALE`). (3) A `table` grows DOWN from `at`: put `at` at its top-left, or the title block falls off the sheet. (4) A viewport can be re-scaled later by opening the saved DXF: `run plan.dxf --cmd 'layout {"option":"set","name":"PLAN"}' --cmd 'viewport.set {"handle":"10D","scale":50}'`, the handle is in the earlier `mview` result.

## Parametric ECO plate drawing on an A3 sheet at true 1:1 (2026-10-27, 0.13.0 headless; PDF rendered and measured)
> Task: a 2D mounting plate from a parameter file (layers, bolt block, dimensions, title-block attributes, DXF + 1:1 PDF; invented part). The script read `KEY = value` lines (plate size, corner radius, hole margins and spacing, ECO number, revision) and wrote two block files plus the drawing and PDF.
- **Block files, then `insert {"name","file","at"}`:** build the bolt (hex head `polygon {"sides":6,"center":[0,0],"radius":13/sqrt(3),"inscribed":true,"angle":0}` + a Ø8 circle) and the title block (rectangles, lines, texts, `attdef`s) in their own small `--save` runs, then insert them from the files. No `block` handles needed and no stray original at the origin (which `block` would leave behind as an insert). Four bolt inserts, one title-block insert; `info` counted `Block Reference: 5`.
- **`insert {"file","attribs":{"ECO":"ECO-2026-089","REV":"Rev B"}}` sets attribute values on a file-defined block** (tags are the attdef tags, upper case; `attdef` default text shows for tags not listed). Render: the values appear in their cells; texts that are too long for a cell run into the next one (a 10-character date at height 4.5 overlapped the next cell: use height 3.8 for narrow cells).
- **Layers and weights:** `layer.new {"name","color":7|"red"|"blue","lineweight":0.5,"linetype":"CENTER"}` (load the linetype first); the PDF shows 0.5 mm on the plate outline, red CENTER dashes, blue dimensions. The thick outline made the bolt's two concentric circles merge into a blob: give block geometry its own thinner weight with `properties.set {"handles":[…],"lineweight":0.25}` (handles of a fresh drawing start at `100`).
- **Rounded plate:** `rectang` then `fillet {"handle":"{last}","polyline":true,"radius":12.5}` (4 corners) is one closed polyline.
- **Dimensions:** `dimstyle {"name","DIMTXT":3.5,"DIMASZ":2.5,"DIMEXO":1.5,"DIMEXE":2,"DIMTXSTY":"SCH","current":true}`, `dimlinear {"p1","p2","at"[,"rotation":90]}` for width, height and the centre-to-centre 200 and 100. **`DIMDEC` did not change the text and `dimradius` printed `R13` for 12.5**; fix with `textedit {"handle":"{last}","text":"R12.5"}` straight after the `dimradius {"center","point","at"}` (the point must lie on the arc). **`dimradius` on a fillet puts its line through the arc centre, which sits close to the nearby hole**: dimension a corner that is far from the hole leader. `mleader {"points":[[arrow],[bend],[landing]],"text":"4X M8 TAPPED\\P(TAP DRILL 6.8)"}` takes `\P` for a second line.
- **A normal `exportpdf` with the default `fit` is NOT 1:1:** the A3 sheet came out 1.018 times too big (frame 407.1 mm instead of 400, plate 254.7 mm). `exportpdf {"path","paper":"A3","landscape":true,"scale":"1:1","fit":false,"center":true}` gave frame 400.06 mm, plate 250.1 mm and equal 10 mm margins (page 1190.55 x 841.89 pt). `center:false,"offset":[0,0]` shifted the sheet by about -3.6/+3.3 mm. Measure it: `gs -sDEVICE=pnggray -r72`, scan a row for the first and last dark pixel (pixels = points; mm = pt / 2.8346).
- `convert x.dxf x.dwg` kept all counts (4 circles, 5 dimensions, 5 block references, 1 multileader, 2 polylines, 5 texts).
